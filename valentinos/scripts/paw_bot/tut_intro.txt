PAW-BOT: Welcome to the Wiring Tutorial. By the end of this, you will have built two vehicles from scratch, and you will know how to wire, rewire, and use the meters.

PAW-BOT: We will start with Vehicle 1 — Obstacle Avoidance. It uses two infrared range sensors (RL and RR) wired directly to two motors (FL and FR).

PAW-BOT: Press "Open Wiring Editor" to begin. Inside the editor, click RL — the left range sensor — then click FR — the right forward motor. That draws your first wire.

PAW-BOT: Then connect RR to FL. When both wires are in place, press Done.
