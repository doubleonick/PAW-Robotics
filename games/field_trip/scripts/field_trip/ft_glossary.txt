(1)virtual fields: a physics based metaphor used for how environmental sources relate to the motors of a robot through its sensors.  Sources are treated as having magnetic or gravitational like forces, pulling or pushing on the robot in ways that influence its motion in useful ways for navigating the environment.
(2)Seek: the type of motion that results from one or more sensors telling the robot that there is a source "pulling" on it like a magnet to a piece of metal, or a second magnet with an opposing polarity.
(3)Flee: the type of motion that results from one or more sensors telling the robot that there is a source "pushing" it away like a magnet of the same polarity as the robot itself.
(4)Orbit: the type of motion that results from one or more sensors treating the robot as an object to be pulled into an orbit around a source.  The orbit is not perfectly circular or elliptical and may decay inward or outward.
(5)Flow-around: the type of motion that results from one or more sensors letting the robot behave in a manner similar to light being bent around a massive object by gravitational lensing.  Or, if you prefer, it is like the robot is a log riding the current of a river, and flowing around a rock in its path.
